Privacy Policy

Quedo · Last updated: 19 September 2026

In short: only the parent has an account in Quedo. A child never signs up, never gives an email address, never logs in. On the free tier, nothing about your child leaves the phone — their name, age, chores and entire history stay on the device. That changes only if you explicitly turn sync on. No ads, no advertising identifiers, no tracking of children.

1. Who wrote this

Quedo is an Android app developed by Knight Baryon (Erdoğan Ersin Keskin, Istanbul, Türkiye). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Contact: quedo@knightbaryon.com

2. What we process

For every user — the parent's account

Using the app requires a parent account. For that we process:

DataWhy
Email addressTo identify your account and let you sign in
Password (only a cryptographic hash)Sign-in verification. The password itself is never stored anywhere
Google account identifier (if you use Google Sign-In)The same purpose. We receive authentication information only

This is your data, not your child's.

On the free tier — nothing about the child is sent anywhere

While you use the app for free, all of the following stays on your phone only and is never sent to any server:

  • Your child's name and age
  • The chores and rewards you define
  • Completed chores, coins earned, the entire history
  • Your parent PIN

If you uninstall the app, this data is removed from your phone and we hold no copy of it.

If you turn sync on — this changes

If you buy premium and explicitly tick the sync consent box, the data above starts being stored on our server so it can be shared between your child's device and yours: the child's name, age, chores, rewards and activity history.

This step is spelled out in the app, and the purchase button stays disabled until the consent box is ticked. You can withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Premium); sending stops when you do, and your purchase stays valid.

What we do not collect

  • No ads, no advertising identifiers, no ad tracking
  • No location data
  • No access to photos, camera, microphone or contacts
  • No profiling of a child's behaviour and no marketing use of it
  • No selling of data to third parties, under any circumstances

3. Children's data

Quedo is designed for children aged 6-11 to use, but a child is never the account holder. A child does not register, and gives no email or password. If a child has their own phone, it joins the family as a device using an invite code the parent shows them — that is not an account and there is no personal information behind it.

Data about a child can reach our server only through the parent's deliberate consent (see above). The parent gives that consent and can withdraw it at any time.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use only the service providers the app needs to function:

ServiceWhat forWhat it sees
Supabase Account management and, only when sync is on, data storage Your email address; your family data if sync is on
Google Google Sign-In, app distribution, purchases Authentication information if you choose Google Sign-In; the purchase transaction
Adapty Purchase infrastructure Only the parent's account identifier. No field about a child is ever sent. It never runs on a child's phone, and on a parent's phone it starts only after the upgrade screen is opened

5. Where data is stored

Server-side data is hosted within the European Union (Supabase, Frankfurt, Germany). On the free tier only your account information is there in the first place.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it and all family data attached to it are erased permanently and immediately — no archival copy is kept.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK (Law No. 6698) you have the right to:

  • Know what data about you is processed
  • Have it corrected
  • Have it erased — see below
  • Object to processing
  • Receive a copy of your data

To exercise any of these, write to the address above.

8. Deleting your account and data

From inside the app: Settings → Delete account. Once you confirm, your account, family, child profiles, chores, rewards and the whole history are permanently erased.

If you cannot reach the app: see the account deletion request page.

9. If this policy changes

If the app's data behaviour changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. Significant changes are announced in the app.

10. Contact

For questions or requests: quedo@knightbaryon.com

If you are in Türkiye and wish to complain to the data protection authority: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu (kvkk.gov.tr).
