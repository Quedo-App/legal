Deleting your account

Quedo · Last updated: 19 September 2026

Fastest route: from inside the app

Open the app → Settings → Delete account

The screen spells out what will be erased and asks you to confirm. The moment you confirm, it is done — you do not need to write to us as well.

If you cannot reach the app

If you have lost your phone, already uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in, you can request deletion by email:

quedo@knightbaryon.com

Send it from the address registered to your account, with "Account deletion request" as the subject. Sending from the registered address is how we confirm the account is really yours — requests from another address may need additional verification.

We complete requests within 30 days at the latest and tell you when it is done.

Exactly what gets erased

Account deletion cannot be undone. All of the following is permanently erased:

  • Your account and email address
  • Your family and the child profiles in it (name, age)
  • Every chore and reward you defined
  • Completed chores, coins and the entire activity history
  • The link between any paired child devices and the family

No archive or backup copy is kept.

The one thing that is not erased

Your purchase record stays with Google Play. That is not ours to delete — the purchase is a transaction between you and Google, and Google keeps it for its own retention periods. That record is also what lets you restore your purchase if you reinstall the app with the same Google account.

For refunds or questions about the purchase record, you need to contact Google Play support.

Data on your phone

On the free tier all of the data is on your phone in the first place. Uninstalling the app removes it. Account deletion also wipes the local data on the device you used.
